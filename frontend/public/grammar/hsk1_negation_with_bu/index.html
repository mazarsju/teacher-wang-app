To say that something is not true, put 不 (bù) before the verb.

The rest of the sentence stays in the same order: Subject + 不 + Verb + Object.

This is the one word you need to say no to something — without it you can only agree, never disagree or correct someone.

Structure

Subject + 不 + Verb + Object

我喜欢茶。 (Wǒ xǐhuan chá.) I like tea. → 我不喜欢茶。 (Wǒ bù xǐhuan chá.) I do not like tea.

TIP不 negates almost everything — except 有, which always takes 没 instead.

Examples

我不喜欢茶。 Wǒ bù xǐhuan chá.

Breakdown: 我 (I) · 不 (not) · 喜欢 (like) · 茶 (tea)

→ I do not like tea.

我不认识他。 Wǒ bú rènshi tā.

Breakdown: 我 (I) · 不 (not) · 认识 (know a person) · 他 (him)

→ I do not know him.

她不学习中文。 Tā bù xuéxí Zhōngwén.

Breakdown: 她 (she) · 不 (not) · 学习 (study) · 中文 (Chinese)

→ She does not study Chinese.

我不知道。 Wǒ bù zhīdào.

Breakdown: 我 (I) · 不 (not) · 知道 (know a fact)

→ I do not know.

我不是老师。 Wǒ bú shì lǎoshī.

Breakdown: 我 (I) · 不 (not) · 是 (am) · 老师 (teacher)

→ I am not a teacher.

他不会说中文。 Tā bú huì shuō Zhōngwén.

Breakdown: 他 (he) · 不 (not) · 会 (can/know how to) · 说 (speak) · 中文 (Chinese)

→ He cannot speak Chinese.

我不想喝茶。 Wǒ bù xiǎng hē chá.

Breakdown: 我 (I) · 不 (not) · 想 (want to) · 喝 (drink) · 茶 (tea)

→ I do not want to drink tea.

我不去。 Wǒ bú qù.

Breakdown: 我 (I) · 不 (not) · 去 (go)

→ I am not going.

她不来。 Tā bù lái.

Breakdown: 她 (she) · 不 (not) · 来 (come)

→ She is not coming.

你不对。 Nǐ bú duì.

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 不 (not) · 对 (correct/right)

→ You are not right.

Common Mistakes

Using 不 to negate 有

- Incorrect: 我不有水。 (Wǒ bù yǒu shuǐ.) - Correct: 我没有水。 (Wǒ méiyǒu shuǐ.) — I do not have water.

有 (have) is always negated with 没(有), never 不 — even though English "don't have" tempts you to say 不有.

Using 没 for a state or preference

- Incorrect: 我没喜欢茶。 (Wǒ méi xǐhuan chá.) - Correct: 我不喜欢茶。 (Wǒ bù xǐhuan chá.) — I do not like tea.

没 negates completed actions. 喜欢 is a state, not a completed action, so it takes 不.

What Can You Say Now?

You can now disagree, correct someone, or say that something is not the case — 我不喜欢茶 (I don't like tea), 我不是老师 (I'm not a teacher) — just by putting 不 right before the verb. Try describing your day out loud and negate one sentence with 不 to practice.

Quick Summary
