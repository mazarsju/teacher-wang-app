To ask a yes-no question, keep the same word order as a statement and add 吗 (ma) at the end.

You do not move the verb or add English-style helping words like "do" or "does".

Once you know this, you can turn any statement you already know how to say into a question, without learning any new vocabulary.

Structure

Statement + 吗？

你喜欢茶。 (Nǐ xǐhuan chá.) You like tea. → 你喜欢茶吗？ (Nǐ xǐhuan chá ma?) Do you like tea?

TIPKeep the statement exactly as it is and just tack 吗 onto the end — no reordering, no English-style "do/does".

Examples

你是学生吗？ Nǐ shì xuésheng ma?

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 是 (am/is/are) · 学生 (student) · 吗 (question particle)

→ Are you a student?

他是老师吗？ Tā shì lǎoshī ma?

Breakdown: 他 (he) · 是 (am/is/are) · 老师 (teacher) · 吗 (question particle)

→ Is he a teacher?

你忙吗？ Nǐ máng ma?

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 忙 (busy) · 吗 (question particle)

→ Are you busy?

你好吗？ Nǐ hǎo ma?

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 好 (well/good) · 吗 (question particle)

→ How are you? (Are you well?)

你累吗？ Nǐ lèi ma?

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 累 (tired) · 吗 (question particle)

→ Are you tired?

你高兴吗？ Nǐ gāoxìng ma?

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 高兴 (happy) · 吗 (question particle)

→ Are you happy?

你在家吗？ Nǐ zài jiā ma?

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 在 (at/to be located) · 家 (home) · 吗 (question particle)

→ Are you at home?

我可以喝茶吗？ Wǒ kěyǐ hē chá ma?

Breakdown: 我 (I) · 可以 (can/may) · 喝 (drink) · 茶 (tea) · 吗 (question particle)

→ May I drink tea?

我对吗？ Wǒ duì ma?

Breakdown: 我 (I) · 对 (correct/right) · 吗 (question particle)

→ Am I right?

Common Mistakes

Putting 吗 first

- Incorrect: 吗你喜欢茶？ (Ma nǐ xǐhuan chá?) - Correct: 你喜欢茶吗？ (Nǐ xǐhuan chá ma?) — Do you like tea?

Stacking 吗 onto a question that already has a question word

- Incorrect: 你喜欢什么吗？ (Nǐ xǐhuan shénme ma?) - Correct: 你喜欢什么？ (Nǐ xǐhuan shénme?) — What do you like?

English marks every question with a "?" — but 吗 only belongs on yes-no questions. A question word like 什么 already makes the sentence a question, so 吗 never joins it.

What Can You Say Now?

You can now ask a yes-no question about anything you can already say as a fact, just by adding 吗 to the end — 你喜欢茶吗？ (Do you like tea?), 他有水吗？ (Does he have water?). Try it out loud: take a sentence you already know and turn it into a question by adding 吗.
