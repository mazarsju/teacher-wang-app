几 (jǐ) and 多少 (duōshao) both ask how many / how much.

几 (jǐ)多少 (duōshao)
Expected sizesmall numberany amount, often larger
Measure wordrequired (几杯, 几个)usually not needed
Example几杯茶 (jǐ bēi chá) — how many cups of tea多少钱 (duōshao qián) — how much money

几 and 多少 let you ask about quantities — how many cups, how many people, how much of something — which you will need the moment numbers come up.

Structure

Subject + Verb + 几 + Measure Word + Noun？

你有几杯茶？ (Nǐ yǒu jǐ bēi chá?) How many cups of tea do you have?

Subject + Verb + 多少 + Noun？

他喝多少水？ (Tā hē duōshao shuǐ?) How much water does he drink?

TIPExpecting a small number? Use 几 with a measure word. Expecting any amount, big or small? Use 多少 with no measure word.

When NOT to Use 几

Do not use 几 for amounts that could be large or open-ended, like money — use 多少 instead, and note that 多少 does not need a measure word.

✓ 这个多少钱？ Zhège duōshao qián? (How much is this?) ✗ 这个几钱？ Zhège jǐ qián?

✓ 学校有多少学生？ Xuéxiào yǒu duōshao xuéshēng? (How many students does the school have?) ✗ 学校有几个学生？ Xuéxiào yǒu jǐ ge xuéshēng?

Examples

你几岁？ Nǐ jǐ suì?

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 几 (how many) · 岁 (years old)

→ How old are you?

这个多少钱？ Zhège duōshao qián?

Breakdown: 这个 (this) · 多少 (how much) · 钱 (money)

→ How much is this?

你家有几个人？ Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ ge rén?

Breakdown: 你家 (your family) · 有 (has) · 几个 (how many) · 人 (people)

→ How many people are in your family?

你有几本书？ Nǐ yǒu jǐ běn shū?

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 有 (have) · 几本 (how many books) · 书 (books)

→ How many books do you have?

你有几张纸？ Nǐ yǒu jǐ zhāng zhǐ?

Breakdown: 你 (you) · 有 (have) · 几张 (how many pieces) · 纸 (paper)

→ How many pieces of paper do you have?

Common Mistakes

Moving 几 to the front

- Incorrect: 几你有茶？ (Jǐ nǐ yǒu chá?) - Correct: 你有几杯茶？ (Nǐ yǒu jǐ bēi chá?) — How many cups of tea do you have?

Skipping the measure word after 几

- Incorrect: 你有几茶？ (Nǐ yǒu jǐ chá?) - Correct: 你有几杯茶？ (Nǐ yǒu jǐ bēi chá?) — How many cups of tea do you have?

What Can You Say Now?

You can now ask about quantities — how many cups of tea someone has, how many students are in a class, how much something costs — using 几 with a measure word for small numbers and 多少 for larger or open-ended amounts. Try asking 你有几个...？ about anything you can count around you.

Quick Summary

几 + measure word for a small "how many". 多少 for "how many / how much".

Word

几 - ji3

(How many; several; a few.)

多少 - duo1 shao

(How much; how many.)

岁 - sui4

(Years old; classifier for age.)

钱 - qian2

(Money; a coin; a surname.)

个 - ge4

(A; a piece of (general measure word used before nouns).)

人 - ren2

(Person; people)

本 - ben3

(Root; origin; this; the current one; classifier for books.)

杯 - bei1

(Cup; classifier for a glass or cup of something.)

张 - zhang1

(To open up; to spread; a sheet of paper; classifier for flat objects; a surname.)
