Describe a typical day: when you wake up, what you do in the morning,
afternoon, and evening, and where you go.

## Grammar you can use

- **Time words before the verb** — 今天 / 现在 / 早上 / 晚上 + Subject + Verb,
  or Subject + Time + Verb, e.g. 我早上七点起床。
- **在** (zài) — "Subject + 在 + Place + Verb" to say where something
  happens, e.g. 我在家吃饭。
- **numbers + measure words** — for clock times and quantities, e.g. 七点
  (seven o'clock), 两个朋友 (two friends).
- **几 / 多少** — ask about time or quantity, e.g. 你几点吃饭？
- **什么时候** — ask "when", e.g. 你什么时候上课？

Try to order your sentences roughly by time of day, from morning to night.
