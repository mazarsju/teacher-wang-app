Talk about what you like to do in your free time, why you like it, and
something you did recently.

## Grammar you can use

- **了** (le) — "Subject + Verb + 了 + Object" to mark a completed action,
  e.g. 昨天我看了一本书。
- **想 / 会** (xiǎng / huì) — 想 for what you want to do, 会 for what you know
  how to do, e.g. 我想学做饭, 我会打篮球。
- **也** (yě) — "also", to add a similar hobby, e.g. 我也喜欢跑步。
- **和** (hé) — "and / with", to link people or things, e.g. 我和朋友一起打球。
- **Adjective sentences** — Subject + 很/非常 + Adjective (no 是 needed),
  e.g. 打篮球很有意思。

You can mix a general statement ("I like...") with one specific memory
("Last week I...").
