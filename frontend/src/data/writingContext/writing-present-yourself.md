Write a short introduction of yourself in Chinese: your name, where you are
from, whether you have siblings, and one or two other simple facts about
you.

## Grammar you can use

- **是** (shì) — "Subject + 是 + Noun", e.g. 我是学生。 (I am a student.)
- **有 / 没有** (yǒu / méiyǒu) — "Subject + 有 + Object" to say you have
  something (e.g. siblings), negated with 没有, not 不有.
- **的** (de) — "Owner + 的 + Thing" for possession, e.g. 我的名字 (my name).
- **不** — negate a verb or adjective, e.g. 我不是老师。
- **吗** — turn a statement into a yes/no question if you want to ask
  something back, e.g. 你有兄弟姐妹吗？

Keep sentences simple: one idea per sentence is enough at this level.
